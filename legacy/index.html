Decision rules

Green — run the plan. Progress one variable only.
Yellow — cut contacts 30–50%, bilateral instead of unilateral, drop load/range 10–20%. Do not progress.
Red — no jumping, sprinting, hard cutting or loaded end-range. Contact your clinician if it repeats.

Cut in this order

1. Extra conditioning → 2. High-volume extensive contacts → 3. Decel/landing sets basketball already gave you → 4. Accessory sets → 5. Secondary strength → 6. Primary heavy strength last.
